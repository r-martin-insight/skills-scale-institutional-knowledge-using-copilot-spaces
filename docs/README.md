# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. This documentation centralizes our processes, roles, and best practices to enable consistent, repeatable project execution across all teams. The goal is to turn ideas into delivered work through a clear, lightweight lifecycle — from validating a business need through retrospective and continuous improvement.

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow five key phases:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need, identify stakeholders and champions, define measurable success criteria, and confirm go/no-go before investing in planning. The team produces a Project One-pager capturing the problem statement, objective, success metrics, timeline, risks, and proposed roles.
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, estimate scope, document the Definition of Done, identify dependencies, and prepare a release and milestone plan with a prioritized backlog.
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate using a transparent project board (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done). Daily standups and weekly delivery syncs keep the team aligned on progress, blockers, and risks.
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with pre-release checks, staging verification, post-deploy validation, stakeholder announcements, and rollback readiness.
5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** — Capture learnings, convert action items into tracked improvements, and evolve the process over time rather than keeping it static.

## Roles & Personas

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **Project Manager** | Coordinates schedules, risks, communication, and delivery alignment; owns the risk register and escalation paths. |
| **Product Manager** | Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, measures success, and represents the voice of the customer. |
| **Developer** | Implements features, contributes to planning and estimation, writes tests, and participates in code reviews. |
| **QA / Testing** | Validates quality and acceptance criteria through unit, integration, smoke, security, and manual QA testing. |
| **Stakeholders** | Provide requirements and approvals; receive regular milestone updates and are engaged at key decision gates. |

## Communication Cadence

| Frequency | Event |
|-----------|-------|
| **Daily** | 15-minute team standup — progress, blockers, and dependencies |
| **Weekly** | PM + Product Manager alignment sync; delivery team check-in |
| **Per Sprint / Milestone** | Demo, review, and retrospective |
| **Monthly** | Stakeholder update and milestone summary |
| **As needed** | Blocker escalation, incident response, and risk review |
